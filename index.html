ROGER HAUS

HELLO, HELLO!

I am Roger Haus living in Barcelona. When I was young, brushes and paint were never leaving my pencil case. I learned drawing at the same table where Picasso and Miró once studied (La Llotja — my alma mater). Today, I am on the front page of OpenSea with my Exquisite Workers project, draw fun comics, create stickers and designs for the United Nations.
