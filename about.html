HELLO, I AM ROGER HAUS.

Surrealist illustrator, art director, and digital creator trained at La Llotja in Barcelona — where Picasso and Miró once cultivated their vision.

The Creative Journey

Born and raised in Catalonia, my pencil case was never without fresh brushes and ink. Drawing was not simply a hobby; it was an innate, involuntary mode of perceiving reality. At La Llotja Art and Craft Academy, I developed deep appreciation for classical composition, printmaking, and anatomy.

Over the years, this classical foundation merged with Barcelona's energetic street art movement, graffiti culture, underground comics, and digital automatism.

Nocturnal Automatism & Hypnagogia

My signature method revolves around the hypnagogic state — the borderline consciousness between wakefulness and sleep occurring between 3:00 and 5:00 AM. When the analytical left hemisphere relaxes, involuntary motifs, surreal animal-human hybrids, and geometric labyrinths emerge onto paper.

Exquisite Workers & Digital Art

In 2020, I founded Exquisite Workers, reviving the 1920s Surrealist game of Cadavre Exquis (Exquisite Corpse) for the digital age. Bringing together over 504 international artists, the movement was showcased across OpenSea, digital festivals, and the iconic Picasso Museum of Barcelona.
